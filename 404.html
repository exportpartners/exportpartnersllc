Frequently Asked Questions About Cross-Border E-Commerce

Here you can find answers to common questions about cross-border e-commerce. If you still have questions, we are happy to answer them. Contact us and the Export Partners team will help you with all of your questions.

What Is Cross-Border E-Commerce?

Cross-border e-commerce is an export model in which products or services are sold to buyers in other countries over the internet. Products typically reach customers in global markets through international carriers and online marketplaces.

How Does Cross-Border E-Commerce Work?

In cross-border e-commerce, there are generally two sales methods. What matters is choosing the strategy that fits you best. When selecting the approach that suits your business model, a careful feasibility study is recommended. Many companies struggle because they follow the wrong strategy. With sound planning and budgeting, however, selling internationally online and growing your sales volume is achievable.

What Are the Cost Items in Cross-Border E-Commerce?

Costs can vary depending on the countries and marketplaces where you want to sell. Key cost items to be aware of include marketplace fixed and variable fees, shipping costs, fulfillment fees, digital marketing costs such as advertising, PPC and promotional product giveaways, and storage fees. By calculating your costs accurately, you can set the gross and net profit margin and the selling price that work best for you.

Is Trademark Registration Abroad Necessary?

Registering your trademark abroad gives your brand legal protection in that country. This can help you stop other sellers from using your brand or selling under the same storefront on online marketplaces, which strengthens your brand protection. You can also learn more about international trademark registration and available export support programs.

How Do I Ship Internationally?

You can ship internationally with carriers such as UPS, FedEx and DHL, or through national postal services.

When shipping abroad, the main factors that affect the price are the package's dimensional weight, the shipping service and the delivery time.

Carriers price packages based on dimensional weight, a unit that reflects the volume of a shipment relative to its size. Regardless of the carrier you choose, the higher your package's dimensional weight, the higher the cost.

Dimensional weight is calculated with the formula width x length x height / 5000.

Using your package space efficiently and fitting more units into the same space can help you keep shipping costs to a minimum.

How Is Cross-Border E-Commerce Different from Traditional Exporting?

Traditional exporting mainly works through B2B sales. In cross-border e-commerce, you can target consumers directly, selling B2C to the end customer.

The main difference between the two shows up at customs. Traditional exports usually require a full customs declaration, customs procedures can take around 2-5 business days, and a customs broker is typically involved. Small e-commerce shipments can often move under simplified declarations handled by the carrier, and clearance may be completed within 1-2 days.

Can I Sell on Amazon Without a Company?

Having a registered company is generally needed for financial reasons: to receive invoices when purchasing goods, to invoice your sales and to manage tax matters related to exports. A company in the destination country can also be useful as a local point of contact, since buyer company information may be requested during customs clearance. Proper taxation and income reporting of payouts from international marketplaces will also support your business relationships over the long term.
