How does selling on Amazon UK work?

Amazon.co.uk has been a marketplace separate from Amazon Europe since Brexit: EU VAT, EORI and responsible person registrations do not carry over to the UK, so a separate file is opened for the UK. A company established in your home country can open an account directly; setting up a UK Ltd company is not required, though some sellers prefer it for banking and tax convenience.

Products are shipped to the UK via FBA. A foreign seller holding stock in the UK must register for UK VAT with no threshold; customs entry requires a GB EORI, an importer solution and, depending on the product group, the UKCA mark and a UK-based responsible person. In most cases Amazon collects VAT itself on sales by overseas sellers to consumers; registration is still mandatory.

For brand protection, a UKIPO trademark application opens Brand Registry; an EU (EUIPO) registration is not valid in the UK.

Who is it for?

Sellers with a European account expanding to the UK

Moving your Amazon.de catalog to English listings and GBP pricing, UK VAT and EORI, and separate FBA inventory.

Manufacturers choosing the UK as their first market

English content, one language, one currency, one VAT; a simpler start than continental Europe.

Food and cosmetics manufacturers

Companies setting up in the UK

Forming a Ltd with Companies House and HMRC registrations, and opening the account in the name of the UK legal entity.

Three points specific to the UK

EU registrations do not carry over. An EUIPO trademark, EU EORI, EU responsible person and German VAT cannot be used in the UK. Even if you have a European account, the UK requires a separate set of registrations and separate FBA stock.

Amazon collects the VAT, the registration is yours. In most cases Amazon collects VAT on sales by overseas sellers to UK consumers and pays it to HMRC; even so, a seller holding stock in the UK must register for VAT and file returns.

Cosmetics and food. Cosmetics require a UK-based responsible person and SCPN notification, food requires compliance with UK labeling rules; the steps for selling dietary supplements in the UK are in this guide.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to form a UK company to sell on Amazon UK?

No. A company based outside the UK can open an account on Amazon.co.uk; what is required is UK VAT, GB EORI, a responsible person and an importer solution. A UK Ltd may be preferred for banking and tax convenience.

Is VAT registration mandatory for Amazon.co.uk?

It is mandatory with no threshold for foreign sellers holding FBA stock in the UK. In most cases Amazon collects VAT itself on consumer sales, but the registration and filing obligations remain with the seller.

Is my European account valid in the UK?

No. Since Brexit, Amazon.co.uk is a separate marketplace: separate VAT, separate EORI, separate responsible person, separate FBA stock. Your European catalog can be carried over to English listings, but the registrations are done again.

What is UKCA, and which products need it?

The UK's counterpart to the CE mark, required for regulated product groups such as electronics, toys and PPE. Food and cosmetics do not use UKCA; they follow their own labeling and responsible person rules.

Is my EUIPO trademark valid in the UK?

No. Since 2021 an EU trademark no longer covers the UK; a separate application with the UKIPO is required. We run both applications in the same plan.

UK or Germany: which should I start with?

With one language, one VAT registration and one currency, the UK is often a simpler starting point for many manufacturers; Germany is the larger market and opens access to 8 countries through Pan-EU. The decision is made in the first call based on product group, target volume and compliance cost.

Can food supplements be sold on Amazon in the UK?

Yes, with a label and ingredient list that meet UK food supplement rules; some ingredients may require additional documents. The steps are covered in our guide to selling food supplements on Amazon UK.

What do cosmetics need in the UK?

A UK-based responsible person, an SCPN (Submit Cosmetic Product Notification) filing and UK labeling rules; an EU CPNP notification is not valid in the UK.
