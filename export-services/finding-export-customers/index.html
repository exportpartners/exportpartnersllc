Export Buyer Sourcing System: Buyer Lists, Email, Meetings

An export buyer sourcing system is the infrastructure that lets a manufacturer reach overseas buyers in its own name: a target buyer list, email sending infrastructure, message sequences, a meeting calendar and reporting. Export Partners is a team that builds this system for manufacturers, runs it together with you in the first months and then hands it over to your own team; the buyer relationship and the sales stay in your name and under your ownership.

What does a system mean, and how is it different from a middleman?

The classic route was trade fairs, distributors or agents: the relationship with the buyer stays with the middleman, and control over price and brand is lost. With a system approach, the company reaches the importer directly: a buyer list drawn from customs and trade data, emails sent from the company's own domain, meetings with buyers who reply, then quotes and samples. There is no middleman; what exists is a process and the tools that run it.

We are not a middleman or an agent. We build the system, run the first cycles with you and hand a working setup over to your team. Companies that want ongoing management can continue with us, but the goal is for finding export buyers to become the company's own in-house work.

How buyer lists are built from data and how cold emails are written is explained step by step in our blog posts; this page describes the service that sets up that work on your behalf.

Who is it for?

Manufacturers dependent on trade fairs and distributors

If new customers only come from trade fairs or middlemen, the system adds a channel that works 12 months a year.

SMEs without their own export team

A measurable, transferable setup without hiring a full-time export manager; one person on your team takes it over.

Brands that want a B2B channel alongside Amazon

Marketplace sales bring retail, the system brings wholesale buyers; the two run in parallel in the same country.

Companies looking for buyers to enter the U.S.

U.S. customs data shows the importer by name; the FDA and customs side is handled by the same team.

Scope of the setup: six parts

Target buyer list

A list built from customs and trade data, importer records and marketplace seller lists, filtered by product group, country and volume, with the decision maker verified.

Email infrastructure

Separate sending domains and mailboxes, warm-up, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, with deliverability measurement. Your main domain stays protected.

Message sequences

English email sequences written for your product group, then measured and refined. No promises or pressure, just enough information for a buyer to reply.

Meetings and quotes

Meeting scheduling with buyers who reply, a buyer file before each meeting, and the quotation and sample process. Either you or we can run the meeting; the decision is yours.

CRM and reporting

Every buyer, message and reply in one dashboard, plus a weekly report covering sends, opens, replies, meetings and quotes. The numbers stay in your account.

Handover

Once setup is complete, your team receives training and a written process. Monthly management can continue if you choose.

How is the system set up in four steps?

01

Target and list

We define the product group, target country and buyer profile, build the first list from data and verify the decision makers. 1 to 2 weeks.
02

Infrastructure and messaging

We set up the sending domain, mailboxes and authentication records, start warm-up and write the first message sequences. 2 to 3 weeks, with warm-up running in parallel.
03

First cycle

Sending begins, replies are sorted and meetings are scheduled. Messages and lists are adjusted based on the measurements. 4 to 8 weeks.
04

Handover or continue

The working system is handed over to your team with a written process. Companies that want monthly management can continue with us.

Frequently asked questions

How do you find export customers?

There are three routes: trade fairs, middlemen and direct outreach. In direct outreach, an importer list is drawn from customs and trade data, the decision maker is verified, contact is made by email from the company's own domain, and meetings are held with those who reply. The system builds this third route.

Where does the international buyer list come from?

U.S. customs records show the importer by name; for other countries, trade databases, importer associations and marketplace seller lists are used. The list is filtered by product group and volume, and email addresses are verified.

Does cold email really work?

If the list is accurate and the message gives the buyer useful information, replies come in; the rate varies by industry and product, so we do not promise, we measure. To avoid putting your main domain at risk, sending is done from a separate domain.

Who runs the meetings?

The decision is yours. In the first cycle we conduct the meetings together; if your team is ready we hand over, and if you prefer, the meeting and quoting step stays with us.

How long does it take?

From setup to a working system usually takes 3 months: list and infrastructure in the first 3 to 5 weeks, the first sending cycle in 4 to 8 weeks. First meetings generally start in the second month; this varies by industry.

Are you a broker or sales agent?

No. We do not sell on commission; we build the system and teach you how to run it. The buyer relationship and pricing stay with you.

Can this work alongside Amazon?

Yes. Amazon brings retail sales and this system brings wholesale buyers; the two channels run in parallel in the same country, sharing the brand and compliance file.

Which countries?

We start with the U.S. because data access is strongest there; for Europe and the Gulf, trade databases are used. Country selection is made at the first meeting based on your product group and current exports.
