Setting up a limited company in the UK: who it is for, what you need, what it costs

The most common structure for a UK company managed from abroad is a single-shareholder private limited company (Ltd). The director does not need to live in the UK; being at least 16 years old is enough. It is set up by manufacturers and brands that want to sell on Amazon UK, invoice buyers in the UK, or build a second company structure outside the EU.

Identity verification: mandatory since 18 November 2025

Anyone to be registered with Companies House as a new director or person with significant control (PSC) must first verify their identity. Directors living abroad can do this with a biometric passport through GOV.UK One Login or through an authorised corporate service provider (ACSP); the personal code issued at the end is entered on the application. In existing companies, directors complete verification with their next Confirmation Statement, and that statement cannot be filed until all directors are verified.

UK company formation cost

The Companies House online incorporation fee has been 100 GBP since 1 February 2026; the annual Confirmation Statement fee is 50 GBP, and an application to strike the company off the register is 13 GBP. On top of these come the registered office and director service address, annual accounting and filing support, and periodic returns if the company is VAT registered. A written quote is provided based on the company structure and sales channel.

Frequently asked questions

Can a non-resident set up a limited company in the UK?

Yes. The director does not need to live in the UK; being at least 16 years old is enough. The registered office and director service address are provided as a service, and the application is filed remotely. Since 18 November 2025, directors must complete identity verification before the application.

What does Companies House registration mean?

Companies House is the United Kingdom’s company registry. “Registration” means entering the company on this register, in other words the incorporation itself; once it is complete, a company number and a Certificate of Incorporation are issued.

How does identity verification work?

A director living abroad verifies their identity with a biometric passport through the GOV.UK One Login app or through an authorised corporate service provider (ACSP) registered with Companies House. The personal code issued at the end is used on the incorporation application and on later filings.

How long does it take to set up a company in the UK?

Companies House online applications are usually decided within 24 hours. The corporation tax reference (UTR) usually arrives by post within 14 days of incorporation; VAT registration can take several weeks depending on the application and document process.

What makes up the cost of setting up a UK company?

Official fees: online incorporation fee 100 GBP, annual Confirmation Statement 50 GBP. Service items: registered office and director service address, mail forwarding, annual accounting and filing follow-up; if the company is VAT registered, periodic returns are added.

Can a PO box be used as the registered office address?

No. The registered office must be an "appropriate address" where documents sent to the company can reach someone on its behalf; Companies House does not accept PO box addresses. Since 4 March 2024, a registered email address must also be provided; this address is not made public.

Which filings are mandatory after the company is formed?

Each year, a Confirmation Statement (within 14 days after the review period) and annual accounts go to Companies House; first accounts are due within 21 months of incorporation, later accounts within 9 months of the financial year end. The corporation tax return (CT600) is due to HMRC within 12 months of the accounting period, and the tax payment within 9 months and 1 day. If the company is VAT registered, periodic VAT returns also apply.

What is the corporation tax rate in the UK?

19 percent on profits up to 50,000 GBP and 25 percent above 250,000 GBP; marginal relief applies between the two. For short accounting periods and where associated companies exist, the thresholds are reduced proportionally.

When is VAT registration required?

For UK-established businesses, the registration threshold is 90,000 GBP in turnover over 12 months. A company managed from outside the UK is, in most cases, treated as a non-established taxable person (NETP); being incorporated in the UK or using a UK address does not on its own make it established. There is no threshold for an NETP; registration is required once taxable sales in the UK begin.

Who pays VAT on Amazon UK?

For sales from UK stock and for imported sales up to 135 GBP, the marketplace (Amazon) calculates and pays VAT on behalf of the overseas seller. VAT and customs duty on importing the goods into the UK remain the seller's responsibility; this is why VAT registration is still needed for most sellers.

Why does VAT often not come through in low-cost formation packages?

VAT registration is done with HMRC, not Companies House, and HMRC may request documents about sales and the supply chain after the application. Our field observation is that applications made before sales and documents are ready tend to take longer or get rejected; this is why VAT described as "included" in a package often cannot actually be delivered.

Which documents does HMRC request for VAT registration?

For companies selling on Amazon, HMRC may send a questionnaire after the application: seller account opening and payment account emails, store link, sales and inventory reports, FBA shipment summary, supplier and logistics invoices and contracts, import documents, trademark registration, bank statements and documents showing the director's address. Having documents in English and consistent with each other determines the timeline.

Do I need an EORI number?

If goods will move between the UK and another country, a GB EORI is required; companies that sell only services generally do not need one. If goods will also go to the EU, an EU EORI may be needed separately on the EU side.

How do I open a bank account for a UK company?

For companies managed from abroad, digital business accounts are the most common route. Traditional UK banks may ask non-resident directors for additional documents and an in-person meeting; the right route depends on the company structure.

How do I look up a UK company?

This is done on the free Companies House search service using the company name or number; the company's status, directors, registered address and filed documents can be viewed.

How do I close my UK company?

For a company with no activity, a strike off application is made to Companies House, with a fee of 13 GBP. Before that, the final corporation tax return should be filed and debts settled; the application requires the authentication code sent by post to the company address.
